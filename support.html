Support / Destek

English

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: metinduman@outlook.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

Altitude looks wrong? GPS altitude needs open sky; wait a minute after starting and check the GPS quality indicator. You can align to terrain height from the live screen.

Recording stops in the background? Allow location access "Always" in Settings so tracking continues when the screen is off.
